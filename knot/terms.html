1. What Knot is

Knot lets a small group of people — a family, a couple, a few friends — take a photo each day and keep them together as a shared set. A "knot" is one such set of photos. You can also save a knot to your device as an image or a video.

Each permission is optional and can be revoked in iOS Settings. Declining a permission disables only the feature that needs it.
